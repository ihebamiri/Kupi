# Code of conduct

Kupi follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the people using Kupi. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks aren't accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/ihebamiri). Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainer may remove comments or block participants who don't follow this code.
